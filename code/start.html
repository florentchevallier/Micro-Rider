Micro-Rider

A virtual ride companion for your indoor trainer

▶ Launch

Open the guide
  1. Use Chrome, Edge or Opera (GX works too) on a computer. Bluetooth needs one of these; Safari and Firefox won't connect to a trainer.
  2. Close other apps that hold the trainer (Zwift, phone apps, watch). A trainer talks to one app at a time.
  3. Click Launch, then Connect trainer and pick your trainer in the browser's list. Wake it first by pedalling a few turns. Optional: connect heart rate, cadence, or a Zwift Click.
  4. No hardware? Scroll to the bottom of the app and open Simulation (no hardware): sliders stand in for power, cadence and heart rate.
  5. Choose a workout (or No workout for a free ride) and press Start. Pedal: the scenery scrolls with your speed.
  6. The 🕹️ button (top left of the animation) opens the arcade cabinet: games on its screen, arrows + Enter to play, Esc back to the games menu. After 45 s idle it shows a slideshow.

Something wrong? Press Copy report at the top of this page and paste it into your message to me: it holds your browser info and the app's latest log. For Bluetooth problems, the diagnostic pages at the top help to see what the device exposes.
