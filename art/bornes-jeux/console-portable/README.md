# Borne arcade — inspiration console portable

Deux propositions artistiques générées avec l’outil intégré de génération d’images. Référence structurelle : art/commandes-arcade-90/borne-reference.png. Les prototypes existants ne sont pas modifiés.

- classique.png : fenêtre encadrée, pupitre plus haut.
- grand.png : fenêtre plus haute, pupitre abaissé et entourage aminci.
- index.html : comparaison des deux visuels.

Les croix et boutons sont représentés dans ces recherches visuelles. Leur séparation en sprites et la création des états pressés restent à faire après choix de la composition. Ces images ne constituent pas encore un habillage interactif.

## Prompt commun

+Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: Micro Rider illustrated arcade cabinet skin.
Use the supplied BLUE ARCADE CABINET as the STRUCTURAL REFERENCE. Preserve an unmistakable upright arcade machine: tall side cheeks, wide branded top marquee, recessed display, sloped projecting control deck, full pedestal with coin door and feet. Straight-on centered viewpoint, slight downward view onto the control deck exactly like the reference, whole cabinet visible. This is an on-screen decorative arcade frame for a cycling app, NOT a physical product to build.
Reinterpret the cabinet in an original portable-console-inspired theme: warm ivory molded shell panels, graphite grey display bezel, tasteful narrow burgundy/dusty plum accents, dark plum metal screw heads, carefully shaded tactile plastic and hardware. Same premium illustrated product-render quality as the reference. Restrained pleasing color design, rich materials and crisp finish. NO handheld console shape. NO rectangular Game Boy body. NO Nintendo branding, no Game Boy text, no famous game characters.
Marquee exact text 'MICRO RIDER', original bold confident arcade lettering in dark blue/plum on cream with a subtle cycling chainring motif. The display interior must remain a plain very dark navy rectangle for later game compositing, no game screenshot, no UI or reflections obscuring this area. The surface below the screen is an arcade sloped control deck, NOT a handheld front face.
On this deck: a compact BLACK one-piece directional cross at the LEFT, and TWO small deep burgundy round arcade buttons at the RIGHT (similar size to the reference's existing round buttons). A pair of tiny oblong START/SELECT keys may be centered between them. D-pad must follow the perspective of the slanted control deck, not float vertically. Controls need restrained shadows and clean separation, designed to be separate assets later. No joystick shaft, no big control buttons.
Lower pedestal: restrained diagonal speaker slots and an original small cycling emblem plus a discreet coin-door detail; avoid copying the handheld console face layout.
Background: same quiet deep blue studio gradient as reference. Portrait image, aspect 2:3, single complete cabinet, generous narrow side margins, centered composition. Strong actual arcade-cabinet silhouette, no hands, no people, no watermark.


## Variantes

Classique : ouverture de jeu approximativement x24–76 %, y20–58 % de l’image, pupitre autour de y65–76 %, entourage généreux.

Grand : ouverture de jeu approximativement x23–77 %, y16–65 %, pupitre autour de y70–80 %, entourage plus fin, commandes compactes. Proportions artistiques indicatives : repères exacts à relever dans les images retenues.
